# Manual acceptance

Run the automated tests and demo first. Then run the camera and confirm its documented positive case, an empty scene, two simultaneous candidates, poor lighting, partial occlusion, and loss/re-entry. Confirm Q releases the device by subsequently using a different camera app.

Record expected versus detected results and settings, including failures, before claiming camera verification. Do not use a synthetic fixture as evidence for an accuracy percentage. Do not collect or publish images of non-consenting people. Keep personal samples and output reports out of Git. No live camera results are pre-filled here.
